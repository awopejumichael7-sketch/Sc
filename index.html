Questions

Frequently asked

Is Scholar's Camp really free?
Yes. The entire platform runs on free-tier technology, and there is no paid plan required to use it.
How do students get an account?
Only administrators create accounts. Each student receives a Student ID and a passcode, then logs in with their ID and registered phone number.
Does it work without internet?
Yes — install it like an app, and ebooks, the handbook, syllabus, and notes stay available offline. Everything syncs automatically once you're back online.
Which class levels are supported?
Year 5 through Year 12, plus A Level and Advanced students, across all nine subjects.
