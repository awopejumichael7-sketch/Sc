Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my passcode?
Students and teachers should contact their administrator, who can reset it from the Admin dashboard. Administrators can use "Forgot password?" on the admin login page.
Why can't I download videos or audio?
Videos and audio are streaming-only by design, to protect teachers' recorded sessions from redistribution.
How do I ask a question during a live class?
Use the Live Chat room for that course, available from the sidebar under Community.
My AI Tutor isn't responding — why?
The platform automatically tries several free AI providers. If all are temporarily unavailable, wait a minute and try again, or open a support ticket.
Can I use Scholar's Camp offline?
Yes — install it to your home screen, and previously opened ebooks, the handbook, and syllabus stay available without internet.
